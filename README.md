# MeadWestvaco (meadwestvaco)
MeadWestvaco was a global packaging company providing packaging solutions to many of the world's most-admired brands in the personal and beauty care, food, beverage, home and garden, and other industries. In 2015 MeadWestvaco merged with Rock-Tenn to form WestRock, and the MeadWestvaco brand has since been retired. No public APIs or developer resources are documented for the legacy MeadWestvaco entity or for its WestRock successor.
